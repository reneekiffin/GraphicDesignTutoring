FAQ

Questions, answered.

Are sessions in person or online?

All sessions are 100% online via Zoom with screen sharing. We can work together no matter where you are in the world.

What software do I need before our first session?

It depends on what you want to learn. For Adobe lessons you'll need a Creative Cloud subscription (free trials are available). For Figma and Canva, free accounts work fine. For Squarespace, a free trial is enough to start. We can talk through your setup before our first session if you're unsure.

How long are sessions?

Most sessions are one hour, billed at $60/hour. Longer blocks can be scheduled if you have a specific project that calls for it.

Do you teach kids and teens?

Yes. I regularly tutor middle and high school students who are exploring design for the first time or working on coursework. Parents are welcome to sit in on the first session.

I'm a complete beginner — is that okay?

Absolutely. A lot of my students have never opened the software before. We start from the very basics and build from there at your pace.

Can I book a package of multiple sessions?

Yes — most students book recurring weekly sessions. Just reach out and we'll set up a schedule that works for you.

What happens if I need to reschedule?

No problem. Just give me at least 24 hours' notice and we'll find a new time that works.
