About Your Tutor

Hi, I'm Renée

I'm a freelance graphic designer and design educator with over 8 years of industry experience working with global brands and independent studios. I believe everyone has a visual voice — sometimes they just need the right guidance to find it.

My teaching approach is practical, project-based, and paced entirely around you. Whether you're picking up Photoshop for the first time or looking to refine a professional portfolio, I'll create a roadmap tailored to your goals.

  • ✓ BA (Hons) Graphic Design, Leeds Arts University
  • ✓ Adobe Certified Professional — Photoshop & Illustrator

  • ✓ Workshops at Leeds College of Art & online
